------------

Pogo is a popular online gaming platform where players can enjoy card games, puzzles, word games, board games, and other casual games. Although Pogo games are generally easy to access, users can sometimes experience problems such as games not loading, freezing, crashing, displaying error messages, or failing to connect.

A Pogo game may stop working because of several different reasons. The problem could be related to your internet connection, browser, cookies, cache, device, account, game server, or outdated software. Fortunately, many common Pogo problems can be resolved by following a few troubleshooting steps.

This guide explains how to fix Pogo Games when they are not working and provides practical solutions that you can try before contacting support.

Common Reasons Why Pogo Games Stop Working
------------------------------------------

Before troubleshooting, it is useful to understand why a Pogo game might not work properly.

Common causes include:

* An unstable or slow internet connection
* An outdated web browser
* Corrupted browser cache or cookies
* Browser extensions interfering with the game
* JavaScript being disabled
* Temporary website or server problems
* Outdated device software
* Problems with the Pogo or EA account
* Security software blocking game content
* A problem specific to one particular Pogo game

Identifying the possible cause can help you choose the most appropriate solution.

Step 1: Check Your Internet Connection
--------------------------------------

The first thing you should check is your internet connection.

Pogo games require a stable internet connection to load game content and communicate with the Pogo servers. If your connection is slow or repeatedly disconnects, the game may remain stuck on the loading screen or fail to start.

Try opening another website in the same browser. If other websites are also loading slowly, your internet connection may be the problem.

You can try the following:

* Restart your Wi-Fi router.
* Disconnect and reconnect to your Wi-Fi network.
* Move closer to your wireless router.
* Try using a wired connection if available.
* Temporarily disconnect other devices using a large amount of bandwidth.
* Try another network to determine whether the problem is connection-related.

After restoring your connection, reload the Pogo game and check whether it works.

Step 2: Refresh the Pogo Game
----------------------------

Sometimes a game may fail to load because of a temporary browser or connection problem.

Start by refreshing the Pogo page.

On most computers, you can use the browser's refresh button or press ``Ctrl + R`` on Windows. On a Mac, you can use ``Command + R``.

Wait for the page to reload completely before launching the game again.

If a normal refresh does not work, close the Pogo tab, open a new browser tab, sign in again, and try launching the game.

Step 3: Clear Browser Cache and Cookies
---------------------------------------

A corrupted or outdated browser cache can sometimes cause websites and games to behave incorrectly.

Browsers save temporary files, images, cookies, and other information to make websites load faster. However, these stored files can occasionally become outdated or corrupted.

Clearing the cache and cookies can help resolve problems such as:

* Games failing to load
* Blank game screens
* Repeated loading screens
* Missing graphics
* Unexpected errors
* Games freezing shortly after opening

Open your browser's settings and look for the privacy or browsing-data section. Select the option to clear cached files and cookies.

After clearing the data, restart the browser and sign in to Pogo again.

Keep in mind that clearing cookies may sign you out of websites, so make sure you know your login information before doing so.

Step 4: Update Your Web Browser
------------------------------

An outdated browser can cause compatibility problems with modern websites and online games.

Make sure you are using a current version of a supported browser. Popular browsers include:

* Google Chrome
* Microsoft Edge
* Mozilla Firefox
* Safari

Open your browser's settings and check for available updates. Install the update if one is available, then restart the browser.

After updating, visit Pogo again and test the game.

Keeping your browser updated is also important for security, performance, and compatibility with modern web technologies.

Step 5: Disable Browser Extensions Temporarily
----------------------------------------------

Browser extensions can sometimes interfere with online games.

Ad blockers, privacy extensions, script-blocking tools, security extensions, and other add-ons can prevent certain game components from loading correctly.

If Pogo is not working, temporarily disable your browser extensions and reload the game.

If the game starts working after disabling the extensions, enable them one at a time to identify which extension is causing the problem.

You do not necessarily need to permanently remove an extension. In many cases, you may be able to add Pogo to the extension's allowed or trusted websites.

Step 6: Make Sure JavaScript Is Enabled
---------------------------------------

Many modern websites and browser-based games rely on JavaScript.

If JavaScript is disabled or restricted, a Pogo game may fail to load correctly or may display an incomplete page.

Check your browser settings and make sure JavaScript is enabled for Pogo.

If you use privacy or security extensions that restrict scripts, check their settings as well.

After making the change, reload the Pogo page and try opening the game again.

Step 7: Try a Different Browser
-------------------------------

If the game still does not work, try opening Pogo in another supported browser.

For example, if you normally use Chrome, test the game in Edge or Firefox.

This is a useful troubleshooting step because it can help determine whether the problem is related to your browser configuration.

If Pogo works normally in another browser, the original browser may have a configuration, extension, cache, cookie, or compatibility problem.

You can then focus on troubleshooting that browser rather than changing your entire device.

Step 8: Restart Your Device
---------------------------

A simple device restart can resolve temporary software problems.

Close Pogo and all other applications, then restart your computer, tablet, or other device.

Restarting clears temporary system processes and can resolve problems caused by applications or browser processes that are no longer responding correctly.

After restarting, open your browser, sign in to Pogo, and test the game again.

Step 9: Check Your Device Software
----------------------------------

Your operating system should also be kept up to date.

If your computer or mobile device is running outdated system software, some websites or applications may not function correctly.

Check for available operating-system updates and install them when appropriate.

You should also make sure that your browser is compatible with the operating system you are using.

After updating your device, restart it and test Pogo again.

Step 10: Check Whether Only One Game Is Affected
------------------------------------------------

It is important to determine whether the problem affects every Pogo game or only one game.

Try opening another Pogo game.

If several games fail to load, the problem may be related to your browser, account, internet connection, or Pogo itself.

If only one game is affected, the problem may be specific to that game.

This simple test can save time because it helps narrow down the possible cause.

Step 11: Check for Temporary Pogo Service Problems
---------------------------------------------------

Sometimes a game may not work because of a temporary problem affecting the service.

If your internet connection works normally, your browser is updated, and multiple Pogo games are failing, the issue may not be caused by your device.

Check official Pogo or EA communication channels for information about service interruptions, maintenance, or known problems.

If there is a widespread service issue, you may need to wait until the problem is resolved rather than repeatedly changing your device settings.

Step 12: Sign Out and Sign Back In
----------------------------------

Account-session problems can occasionally cause unexpected behavior.

Try signing out of your Pogo or EA account and then signing back in.

After signing out:

#. Close the Pogo page.
#. Open a new browser window.
#. Visit the official Pogo website.
#. Sign in again.
#. Launch the affected game.

If the problem was caused by a temporary account session, this may resolve it.

Step 13: Check Security Software
--------------------------------

Antivirus programs, firewalls, VPNs, and other security tools can sometimes interfere with websites or online games.

If you recently installed or updated security software and Pogo stopped working afterward, the software may be affecting the connection.

Instead of permanently disabling security protection, check whether the software provides a safe way to allow the official Pogo or EA website.

Avoid changing security settings unless you understand what the setting does.

If you are unsure, consult the documentation for your security software or contact its official support team.

Step 14: Try a Private or Incognito Window
------------------------------------------

A private or incognito browser window can help determine whether stored browser information or extensions are causing the problem.

Open a private or incognito window and visit the official Pogo website.

Sign in and try launching the game.

If the game works in private browsing mode but not in a normal window, the problem may be related to cached data, cookies, extensions, or browser settings.

Step 15: Check Your Account
---------------------------

If you can access Pogo but cannot play games normally, the issue may be related to your account.

Make sure you are signed into the correct EA or Pogo account.

If you recently changed your password or account information, sign out and sign in again.

For account-recovery or security issues, use the official EA Help resources rather than entering your credentials on an unofficial website.

When Should You Contact Pogo or EA Support?
-------------------------------------------

If you have tried the basic troubleshooting steps and Pogo games still do not work, it may be time to contact official support.

Before contacting support, collect useful information such as:

* The name of the affected game
* Your device type
* Your browser and browser version
* Your operating system
* The exact error message
* When the problem started
* Whether other Pogo games work
* Screenshots of the problem
* Steps you have already tried

Providing these details can help support understand the issue more quickly.

Avoid websites that claim to provide unofficial Pogo technical support or ask you to pay for basic troubleshooting. Use official EA and Pogo support channels whenever possible.

Quick Troubleshooting Checklist
--------------------------------

If you need a quick way to work through the solutions, try these steps in order:

#. Check your internet connection.
#. Refresh the Pogo page.
#. Close and reopen your browser.
#. Clear the browser cache and cookies.
#. Update your browser.
#. Disable browser extensions temporarily.
#. Make sure JavaScript is enabled.
#. Try another supported browser.
#. Restart your computer or device.
#. Install available system updates.
#. Test another Pogo game.
#. Check for service-related problems.
#. Sign out and sign back into your account.
#. Check security software and VPN settings.
#. Contact official EA/Pogo support if the problem continues.

Frequently Asked Questions
---------------------------

**Why is my Pogo game stuck on the loading screen?**

A loading problem can be caused by an unstable internet connection, corrupted browser data, an outdated browser, blocked scripts, browser extensions, or a temporary service problem. Start by refreshing the page and checking your internet connection, then clear your browser cache and update the browser.

**Why does Pogo work in one browser but not another?**

The browsers may have different extensions, cookies, privacy settings, or configurations. If Pogo works in another browser, troubleshoot the original browser by clearing its cache, checking extensions, and updating it.

**Why does only one Pogo game not work?**

If other Pogo games work normally, the issue may be specific to the affected game. Check the game's official support information and try the standard browser troubleshooting steps.

**Can clearing cookies fix Pogo games?**

It can. Outdated or corrupted cookies may interfere with website sessions. Clearing cookies can sometimes resolve loading and login problems, although you may need to sign in again afterward.

**Should I reinstall my browser if Pogo does not work?**

Reinstalling the browser is usually not the first troubleshooting step. Try updating the browser, clearing cache and cookies, disabling extensions, and testing another browser first.

Conclusion
----------

When Pogo Games are not working, the problem can often be resolved without advanced technical knowledge. Start with simple solutions such as checking your internet connection, refreshing the page, clearing browser data, updating your browser, and restarting your device.

If those steps do not help, test another browser and another Pogo game to determine whether the problem is related to your browser or a particular game. You should also consider temporary service issues and account-related problems.

For persistent problems, use official Pogo and EA support resources and provide detailed information about the issue. Avoid unofficial customer-service websites and suspicious support numbers.

By following these troubleshooting steps in order, you can identify many common causes of Pogo game problems and get back to playing with minimal interruption.
